# Gaceta

A showcase theme of Micelio: every page is set like a broadsheet newspaper. There is no illustration and no brand: the look comes from type and make-up alone, one serif, small capitals, old-style figures, thin and double rules, and one stamp of aniline violet. It shares nothing with the look of `bogota`, `espora` or `cartel`, to show how far the same engine and the same content go.

- **Modes:** `prensa` (the default: cold grey newsprint, not cream, with a warm black key plate), `archivo` (an old edition gone yellow, printed in sepia) and `microfilm` (dark: the negative on the reader of a newspaper library, light text on a grey-green ground). The accent is the same aniline violet in all three, darker on paper and lighter on the film. The categories are muted printing inks (violet, umber, graphite, plum, ochre); the theme has no green, orange, vermilion or brand blue, so success is lead grey and always comes with its glyph and its word.
- **Layout:** `centered` header (the masthead), `index` home, `list` post list, `centered` article, `minimal` footer. The `index` home has no hero, featured article or topic guide: `ThemeHero` and the topic cards only show in `/_theme` and in topic blocks. It keeps the latest articles, the fediverse block and the newsletter.
- **Font:** one family, Gaceta Serif, for text, titles, labels and the nameplate; code uses the system monospace. It is Source Serif 4 subset to the latin English and Spanish need and renamed, because the original reserves the name "Source" for unmodified copies (OFL 1.1, section 3). The roman keeps the weight axis (400 to 700) at a fixed optical size of 20, with true small capitals (`smcp`, `c2sc`) and old-style figures (`onum`, `tnum`); the italic is one instance at 400. Keeping the optical size axis would add about 48 KB to the roman alone, which the carbon budget of contract 2.2 (`co2Mg`) does not allow: the static pages of the mock measure within 0.5 mg of their limits with the fonts as they are (44.3 KB). `font-fallbacks.css` is hand-tuned against Liberation Serif with the method of the core's `scripts/perf/font-fallbacks.py`.
- **The press:** labels, navigation, chips and buttons in small capitals; running text justified and hyphenated from 720px up, in the language of the page; a three-line drop cap (`initial-letter`, with a floated letter where it is not supported) and a first line in small capitals; `text-wrap: balance` on headlines and `pretty` on paragraphs; `hanging-punctuation` where the browser has it; a double rule over every section, a thick rule round pull quotes and a ■ at the end of an article. Photographs on cards, covers and section media are printed in the key plate (grayscale). Long rich text set `full` width runs in columns with a rule between them, the one place columns help reading. No shadows, no radii (avatars are square portraits) and no gradients.
- **Sections:** stats as the figures of an edition, pricing as the rates of the classified ads, the FAQ as letters to the editor, testimonials as quotations between rules, the CTA as a boxed notice, the newsletter as a coupon to cut out, logos in the key plate.
- **Slots:** `ThemeMark` (a generic nameplate: the site name between two lozenges, no logo), `ThemeIllustration` (a vignette of one ink per category role: a compound rule, an asterism, an aldine leaf, a rosette and a cartouche; a category outside the list gets a dinkus), `ThemeHero` (a front page set without words: frames, columns of type and the rosette), `ThemeDivider` (a thick-and-thin rule round a lozenge, only in the `columns` footer) and `ThemeProgressMarker` (a printer's manicule on the reading bar). All are static: no island, no JavaScript of the theme.
- **Focus:** every focus ring takes the `focus` role (the stamp ink), and `glow-link` is a 2px ring of it, because the core gives comment fields no other focus indicator.
- **Motion:** the ink of an underline settles when a link is approached, under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. Nothing else moves.

The fonts were made from `SourceSerif4[opsz,wght].ttf` and `SourceSerif4-Italic[opsz,wght].ttf` of google/fonts (`ofl/sourceserif4`, version 4.004) with fontTools: subset to Basic Latin and the Latin-1 letters and punctuation of English and Spanish, instanced (roman: `wght` 400–700 at `opsz` 20; italic: `wght` 400 at `opsz` 18), and renamed in the `name` table, as the license asks of a modified version of a font with a Reserved Font Name.
